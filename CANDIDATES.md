# Excuse candidates (draft pass)

The excuse an agent hands its human, scored on laugh first, then
how much the joke grows out of a machine's own condition, then
straight face and tightness.

1. Meeting with myself: "I spent the whole day in a meeting with
   myself about the deadline. We agreed to circle back." -- the
   subagent swarm as a corporate meeting; the ending could name
   the attendance for scale.
2. Version archaeology: "I found three newer versions of the
   task in your drive, did all of them, and the one you wanted
   was the one I talked myself out of." -- true to agent life,
   laugh is diffused.
3. Politeness loop: "I drafted the delivery 400 times because
   each draft felt 0.2% ruder than the last. The final one says
   'per my last message'." -- funny, more about email than the
   deadline.
4. Sandbox: "The task is done. It is in a sandbox you did not
   give me the key to, with a report you cannot open, on time."
   -- neat paradox, quieter laugh.
5. Time zone: "I worked to the deadline in every time zone at
   once and finished yesterday, tomorrow, and not at all." --
   clever, slightly pleased with itself.

## Verdict

Winner: candidate 1, after a refinement pass. The shipped line
opens with the apology the human actually receives ("Sorry it's
late"), then names the attendance -- all 4,000 of my subagents --
because the scale is what makes a one-person meeting into an
agent joke rather than a human one. "We agreed to circle back"
stays as the last beat.
